## 3. About (Company page copy)

**Source:** LinkedIn

Medina Grant Writing & Consulting is a boutique agency helping nonprofits and charities across Canada secure sustainable funding, strengthen relationships with funders, and build lasting impact.

Our focus goes beyond writing grants — we specialize in reviewing proposals, coaching organizations through funder dynamics, and simulating site visits to prepare for the realities of the funding landscape.

With culturally grounded insight and deep sector experience, we help you:

- Write and refine proposals with a funder’s lens
- Build stronger relationships with current and prospective funders
- Strengthen your internal systems and readiness
- Design programs with funder-conscious strategy from the start

We empower you to own your voice — and support you in aligning it with what funders need to see and feel to say “yes.”

### 5.4 FAQs

**Heading:** FAQs

**Why hire a grant writer?**  
Our clients hire us to save time, save money, and build capacity.

**Do you take a percentage if we win the grant?**  
No. All funds awarded to our clients belong to our clients.

**What are the chances of us winning a grant?**  
When it comes to grants, *it depends*.

Decisions are made based on several factors: the amount of funds available, how competitive the applicant pool is, your project’s alignment with funding priorities, and of course, the strength of the application.

Because of this, it’s impossible to guarantee funding, but a well-aligned and well-prepared application will significantly improve your odds.

**How many grants do you have visibility on?**  
Our current database holds over 1,500 grant opportunities for registered charities and non-profits, with nearly 60% based in Canada. We also maintain an internal database that is updated regularly to ensure no relevant opportunity is overlooked for our clients.

**What is your approach to working on projects?**  
Our approach is collaborative. Clients come to us with different levels of readiness, and we adapt to meet those needs. We don’t expect everything to be in place. We guide our clients through the process, keep them involved at every stage, and ensure their organization’s voice is clear and authentic in every application.
