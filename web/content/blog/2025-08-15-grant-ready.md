Most organizations come to grant writing too early — or too late. Readiness is not about having a perfect binder. It is about knowing where you stand so the proposal can tell a clear, fundable story.

## Start with systems, not the form

Funders look for signals that you can deliver. That usually means:

- A clear program description and who you serve
- Basic financials and governance you can share
- Outcomes you can describe in plain language
- Capacity to report if you win

If those pieces are thin, the best next step is often a readiness conversation — not a rushed application.

## Keep your voice authentic

Strong proposals still sound like your organization. Medina’s approach is collaborative: we coach through funder dynamics and keep you involved so the application stays true to your work.

## Practice the conversation

Many grants end with a site visit or a panel conversation. Rehearsing those moments — questions, roles, follow-up — is part of preparing for a “yes.”
